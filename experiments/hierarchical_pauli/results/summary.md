# Smoke-run summary

The JSONL files in this directory are the raw measurements. The smoke run was
executed with Julia 1.13.4, one Julia thread, and PauliPropagation.jl 0.9.2.

Experiment 1 exercised 2–4 qubit local supports and block lengths 2, 4, and 8.
Experiment 2 exercised 5 and 8 qubits, block sizes 1, 2, and 4, and thresholds
1e-5, 1e-3, and 1e-2. Experiment 3 exercised 5 and 8 qubits with bounded
support/column caps. These are feasibility measurements, not a full scaling
sweep.

The raw files retain allocation and term-count data; the plots are generated by
`benchmarks/generate_plots.py`.
