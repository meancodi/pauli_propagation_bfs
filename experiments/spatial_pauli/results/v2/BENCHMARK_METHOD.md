# V2 benchmark method

Run `julia --project=experiments/hierarchical_pauli experiments/spatial_pauli/benchmarks/run_v2.jl` from the repository root. Set `SPATIAL_V2_MODE` to `pilot`, `targeted`, `seeded`, `final`, `coupling`, or `diagnostic`; `SPATIAL_V2_MAX_CASES` bounds a run. Each invocation writes a new timestamped CSV to `raw/` and appends one record per method immediately. `rank_v2.jl` writes a separate small-system Schmidt-rank CSV.

Methods: S0 is the local conventional sparse dictionary reference. `S0_optimized` is the installed PauliPropagation.jl `PauliSum` backend with `min_abs_coeff=0`, `max_weight=Inf`, and single-threaded propagation; it includes conversion and construction in total time. S1 is the exact bipartition, S2 the exact aligned flat leaf factorization, and S3 the exact recursive expression DAG. All use the same circuit instance, parameters, observable, and zero-state expectation convention.

Circuit seeds are determined by fixed numeric family codes, qubit count, layer count, and seed index. They do not depend on the order in which families are listed. Each record contains a SHA-256 circuit and observable fingerprint, all circuit-defining fields, method, partition scheme, and leaf size. `layers` counts the repetitions used by the circuit generator; `gate_count` records the actual gate count. Periodic Ising is a distinct family with the periodic flag set.

Each method receives one warm-up, five timed repetitions with garbage collection before each, and one separate memory-profile pass. Total time includes initialization and all gate work. `median_gate_s` includes aggregation; `median_gate_excluding_aggregation_s` subtracts the separately measured aggregation time for S1/S2. S3's internal aggregation is counted but its time is not separated, so those fields are blank for S3. The upstream `S0_optimized` call is timed end to end; its API combines conversion, construction, and propagation, so its `median_init_s=0` and its gate field is the combined call rather than a separable gate time. It is excluded from the construction-versus-propagation plot. `peak_retained_bytes` is the maximum `Base.summarysize` of the live representation during the separate profile; it is not RSS or a hard peak-memory bound. `allocated_bytes` is the maximum of the five timed allocations. Temporary growth is represented by counts, not bytes. Resource limits are configured in `run_v2.jl`. No preemptive wall-clock timeout is currently enforced; this limitation is explicit rather than labeling a late case as a timeout.

Records use `completed`, `resource_limited`, `timeout`, `error`, or `skipped`. A failed exact S0 reference is recorded as a skipped dependent case with its reason. Failed rows remain in the CSV and must be counted in summaries. A comparison is valid only if the complete configuration and fingerprint match and both methods completed with an acceptable expectation error. Independent seeds remain separate until after pairing. The diagnostic mode adds per-node expression arity, which is a representation count rather than operator Schmidt rank.
